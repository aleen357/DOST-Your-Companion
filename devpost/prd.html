Dost

A small animated companion learns what matters to one software engineer, then appears over other work with a personal break reminder.

Status: approved

The journey

The incubation builds Dost’s context; an honest running-time interval lets the demo show the main payoff.

01 · GUEST

Meet the egg

See quiet blue screen, hatch-effort bar, and Connect.

02 · CONTEXT

Answer 15 questions

Five per session, one at a time; 24 hours between sessions.

03 · HATCH

Meet your Dost

Answers shape the persona and can create a personalized message pack with a local model.

04 · INTERVENE

Dost appears

A small overlay offers a relevant break over other work.

For the demo, incubation waits and running-time intervals are shortened. Dost does not claim to detect actual screen use.

Incubation surface

First use

Start as a guest

Quiet blue background. An egg is central, with a bar above showing effort remaining. Connect opens a compact bottom dialogue.

Question session

One question at a time

Each session has five required answers, mixing selectable options and typed responses. Choice answers are scored; answers can also shape local message generation after incubation.

Between sessions

Wait 24 hours

After session one and two, Connect stays unavailable until the next session is ready. The demo shortens these waits.

Not in this proof

Guest only

Google sign-in, password accounts, and guest-account linking are deferred. The guest's progress and answers remain available across incubation visits.

Incomplete session · unanswered question

The session does not count as complete until all five questions are answered. The 24-hour wait does not begin for an incomplete session.

Scoring and hatch

Selectable answers contribute to persona scores. If the user chooses local generation, the complete answer profile—including typed responses—is processed by Ollama on this PC.

Chill DOST

Green, warm, supportive. Some choices award +2 chill.

Bossy DOST

Orange-red, more direct but caring. Some choices award +3 bossy.

Score resultThe higher selectable-answer total sets the persona.
RandomnessSome choices may add +2 to either persona at random; a final tie is also resolved randomly.
Hatch conditionAll 15 answers are required before DOST hatches.
IntroductionDOST introduces itself after hatching; no further incubation input is required. Character motion is saved for the final build slice.
Local model generation · optional

After the user installs Ollama and downloads the selected model, DOST can create a tailored message pack from the saved profile. Generation runs on this PC. If the user skips or Ollama is unavailable, DOST uses prepared local messages.

Cross-app reminder and response

A compact character intervention appears at the lower-left edge, like the learner's reference, while other work remains visible.

First reminder · a contextual nudge

After Dost has been running for two hours, it appears automatically with a relevant alternative such as a 15-minute walk or drinking water, plus supportive or coding motivation. Demo mode uses a two-minute interval. This is not screen-use detection.

OK · acknowledged

The overlay closes and records that the user listened. At the next normal interval, Dost offers a motivational quote, another goal, or a joke.

Cross · not done

The overlay closes and is treated as not completed. At the next normal interval, Dost asks whether the user did it, in its own persona. If dismissed again, the following interval brings a different suggestion.

Visible controlsOK and cross are available; swiping away also closes the overlay.
Non-blockingThe full screen and underlying work are not taken over.

What success looks like

IncubationComplete three five-question sessions, with the demo advancing through the 24-hour waits.
PersonaSee the egg hatch to a persona based on selectable answers and, with Ollama ready, generate tailored messages from the local answer profile.
ReminderAdvance the app-running interval and see the small overlay over other work.
Follow-upVerify OK and cross each close the overlay and lead to their distinct next message.
CharacterAt the end, see each persona's lightweight idle and reaction animations.
