Build With AI · Product definition

Dost

A small animated companion learns what matters to you through 15 playful questions, hatches as Chill or Bossy, then appears over other work with a personal break reminder.

Status: approved

The journey

The incubation builds Dost’s context; an honest running-time interval lets the demo show the main payoff.

01 · GUEST

Meet the egg

Purple candy room, progress bar, and Connect. No account.

02 · CONTEXT

Answer 15 questions

Five per session, one at a time, mostly playful little scenes; 24 hours between sessions.

03 · HATCH

Let’s Go → Hello

A rainbow egg brews while a local model writes DOST’s lines; then the egg opens.

04 · INTERVENE

Dost appears

A small overlay offers a relevant break over other work.

For the demo, incubation waits and running-time intervals are shortened. Dost does not claim to detect actual screen use.

Incubation surface

First use

Start as a guest

Purple candy background. An egg is central, with a bar showing “15 little moments until hatch”. Connect opens a compact bottom dialogue.

Question session

One question at a time

Single choice, bounded multi-select and short typed answers. Every option question also takes optional words of your own. Typed-only questions can be declined with “Prefer not to share”.

Between sessions

Wait 24 hours

After sessions one and two, Connect shows a countdown until the next session. The demo shortens this to 30 seconds.

Not in this proof

Guest only

Google sign-in, password accounts, and guest-account linking are deferred. Progress survives restarts on this PC.

The three sessions

Your days: daily roles, restorative breaks, reminder topics, how the to-do list feels, late nights. Little scenes: deadlines, a friend’s favour, “got a minute?”, tripping on stairs, one small win for the month. Your kind of friend: pick a coach, a missed alarm, your “stuck” animal, a favourite joke, what a friend does that cheers you up.

Incomplete session · unanswered question

The session does not count as complete until all five questions are answered or declined. The 24-hour wait does not begin for an incomplete session.

Scoring and hatch

Scene options carry hidden points and hints. Typed text never scores, but it does reach the local model, which runs only on this PC.

Chill DOST

Green, warm, supportive. Gentle-support and worry-leaning choices award +2 chill.

Bossy DOST

Orange-red, playful and direct, never shaming. Firm-push and put-it-off choices award +2 bossy.

Score resultThe higher total sets the persona; a tie is resolved randomly.
RandomnessPlayful choices (jokes, a silly voice) add +2 to a random persona.
Model checkIf the model’s reading disagrees with the score, DOST hatches Chill.
FallbackNo Ollama or model, or a failed run: hatch with the score persona and prepared lines, no error.
Let’s Go, step by step

Egg full → Let’s Go → rainbow egg, “Wait, your DOST is coming!” (one to two minutes with the model) → Hello → the egg cracks open → DOST introduces itself. If the window is not focused when it finishes, the taskbar flashes and a Windows notification says “Your DOST is ready!”.

Hatched home

Handheld

A little handheld

On the purple candy background, DOST lives on the roomy screen of a wide, curved-rectangle handheld in its own colour. Feed and Sleep are the two round buttons on the shell.

Feed

A snack

A strawberry, cookie or watermelon drops in; DOST chews in three bites and wiggles with hearts.

Sleep

Five-hour nap

Nightcap and yawn. Every reminder is muted for five hours, in every mode. Wake ends it early; Feed waits until DOST is awake.

Cross-app reminder and response

A compact character intervention appears at the lower-left edge while other work remains visible.

Two lanes

A habit nudge every two hours (a walk, water, a real break) and a light joke or wisdom line about every 50 minutes, with small jitter and a 30-minute gap around habit nudges. Demo mode: two minutes, 50 seconds, 30 seconds. This is running time, not screen-use detection.

OK · acknowledged

The overlay closes. Neither clock resets. The next light message is an encouragement, then light messages return to jokes and wisdom.

Cross · not now

The overlay closes and light messages are muted for five hours (five minutes in demo). Habit nudges continue. If the crossed message was a habit nudge, the next habit slot asks whether you did it; the slot after that returns to a suggestion.

Visible controlsOK and cross. No swipe on desktop.
Non-blockingThe full screen and underlying work are not taken over.

What success looks like

IncubationComplete three five-question sessions, with the demo shortening the 24-hour waits.
HatchLet’s Go, the rainbow brew and Hello lead to the scored persona, with model-written lines when Ollama is ready.
CareFeed plays the snack animation; Sleep mutes reminders and Wake ends it.
ReminderThe small overlay appears over other work on schedule.
Follow-upOK and cross each close the overlay and lead to their distinct next message.

Boundary

Deferred from this proof

The 50-question bank, external accounts and account linking, real screen-time detection, reminder notifications, an in-app Ollama setup guide, more personas, and any cloud AI.

Decided along the way

Generation is part of Let’s Go instead of a separate opt-in screen; setup lives in the README. Feed and Sleep were added after the end-to-end demo. The questions moved to playful scenes (version 3); earlier guests keep their own set.
