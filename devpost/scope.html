Dost

A small animated companion learns what matters to you, then appears over your work with a fitting break reminder.

Status: approved

The idea in one loop

Three incubation visits give Dost context; an app-running interval brings the cross-app reminder.

01 · INCUBATE

Answer for three visits

Five questions per visit, with a 24-hour wait between sessions.

02 · HATCH

Meet your Dost

Fifteen answers shape a persona; consent can create a tailored message pack.

03 · SIMULATE

Continue working

Dost waits for its app-running interval; it does not track screen use.

04 · INTERVENE

Dost appears

A small character overlays other work with encouragement.

Two simple slime looks

Color distinguishes the variants; final motion and the message help express the mood.

The build adds character movement and expressions in its final slice.
Chill Dost · green

Gentle and supportive. Example: “You’re doing okay. Want to take a 15-minute walk?”

Bossy Dost · orange-red

More direct, still caring. Example: “You’ve been at it a while. Drink some water, then get back to it.”

Angry eyes or emoticons alone may read as anger rather than care. Pair a clear expression with movement and words that reveal the intended tone.

Scope at a glance

Now · prove the core idea

Three incubation visits with five light, non-sensitive questions each; all 15 answers are required; persona scoring; hatch; then a small cross-app intervention after an app-running interval. The guest's actual message pack is selected locally. A separate AI preview can generate a sample from fictional data with an in-app disclosure. The intended experience has a 24-hour wait between question sessions, shortened in the demo.

Later · expand the companion

The full 50-question bank, Google or password accounts and guest-account linking, real phone or desktop screen-time detection, system notifications, additional personas, richer personalization, reminder schedules, and private/server-side AI generation.

Cut · outside this proof

Changing office-provided tools and taking over the whole screen. Real system-wide screen-time monitoring is outside the proof; reminders use an honest app-running interval.

Who and why

The first user is the learner, a software engineer who would appreciate a reminder after a two-hour app-running interval to walk for 15 minutes or drink water, plus reassurance and coding motivation. The project connects to wanting less screen time, more exercise and productivity, and ongoing programming-pattern learning for career resilience.

Answers build a chill or bossy score (for example, +2 chill or +3 bossy, with some answers adding +2 randomly to either). The demo uses 15 questions; the larger 50-question bank is later.

Incubation and identity

The first screen has a quiet blue background, an egg, and a bar showing effort left before hatching. The guest answers five questions, one at a time in a bottom dialogue, once per 24 hours. All 15 answers are required. The demo shortens the waits. After hatching, the green chill or orange-red bossy slime appears as a non-full-screen intervention over other work; character animations are added in the final build slice.
